Middle Georgia Regional Commission

Regional governmental planning and service agency serving eleven Middle Georgia counties, including Macon-Bibb. Its services include planning and public administration, economic development and lending, technology, transportation and transit, and the Area Agency on Aging. The governing council establishes policy and, through its Budget, Audit and Personnel Committee, hires the executive director; the executive director hires staff. The 2026–27 officer list identifies Jenna S. Mashburn as chair, Denese Shinholster as vice chair, Kerry Dunaway as secretary and Executive Director Laura M. Mathis as ex-officio treasurer. Macon-Bibb representatives listed on the council roster are Lester Miller, Valerie Wynn, Carlos McCloud, Wallace Herring and Michelle Parks. Sources: https://www.middlegeorgiarc.org/about-us/mission-values/ ; https://www.middlegeorgiarc.org/mgrc-council-2/ ; https://www.middlegeorgiarc.org/mgrc-council-2/officers-and-committees/ ; https://www.middlegeorgiarc.org/about-us/staff/
