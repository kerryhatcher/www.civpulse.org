You can browse public information without signing in. Start with the [community calendar](/calendar/) for events or [Search](/search/) when you know a name or topic.

## Explore your county

The [Bibb County community hub](/counties/bibb/) brings together selected local
maps, organizations, people, elections, and upcoming events. Use the section links
to move around the page, open a district map, or follow an organization to its
profile and calendar. Elections appear newest first and may include past elections.
The full directories and calendar include records outside Bibb County; the hub is
a curated starting point, not a complete county directory.

**Bibb County at a glance** shows 59 U.S. Census Bureau measures covering people,
households and housing, income and work, education and connectivity, health, and
geography. **See the story in the numbers** adds Age, Race & ethnicity, and Poverty
chart tabs. Age bars use a shared 0–10% scale for 18 five-year bands. Race bars
use a 0–100% scale, with Hispanic origin shown separately. Poverty dots show
estimates and lines show their 90% confidence intervals on a 0–50% scale. Exact
values and source links accompany each chart. Arrow keys, Home, and End switch
tabs; without JavaScript, all three charts remain visible.
Six headline figures also lead into three percentage comparisons with
Georgia. The comparison bars share a 0–100% scale and matching 2020–2024 ACS
periods; they are not rankings or tests of statistical significance. Open a topic
under **Explore the county profile** to see Bibb and Georgia figures side by side.
Each measure links to its official Census source and includes its reference
period. **Age breakdown** has 18 non-overlapping five-year bands, from ages 0–4
through 85+, with resident counts and shares. **Race & ethnicity** distinguishes
race from Hispanic origin: Hispanic residents may be of any race, and the White
non-Hispanic row is a subset, so all eight rows must not be added together.
**Poverty** shows 2024 SAIPE rates for all ages, children under 18, and related
children ages 5–17 in families, with 90% confidence intervals. Each rate uses its
own eligible population; the child groups overlap. Georgia comparisons use
the same SAIPE series rather than mixing it with state QuickFacts poverty data.
**Download the data** saves a CSV of all measures, both geographies,
reference periods, source URLs, counts, confidence intervals, definition notes,
and the source-check date. Expand
**About these figures** for methodology and the source-check date. Population
estimates, Census counts, business statistics, and ACS five-year survey figures cover different periods; survey
figures have sampling uncertainty. These are saved figures, not live statistics.
Upcoming public events load when you open the page. More county hubs will follow
after the Bibb County pilot is reviewed.

## Search public meeting documents

Visit the [public document archive](/documents/) to browse published document
titles and filenames. Enter a name, topic or phrase to search titles, filenames
and document contents together. Each document appears once, with matching
passages when available. Leave the search empty to browse, and select **Load
more documents** for additional catalog matches. If content search is temporarily
unavailable, a notice explains that only title and filename matches are shown.
Open the document, its original source, or a related meeting from each result.
Automated transcripts and OCR may contain errors. Unsupported or oversized
originals remain downloadable and show when a searchable derivative is needed.
The archive displays a preparation message until configured; ordinary meeting
attachments remain on their meeting pages.


## Find a meeting and read its notes

Meetings with a recording show a **Meeting video** section. YouTube and Vimeo
play in the page, with fullscreen available. **Watch video on the host's site**
opens the original recording if embedding is restricted or the host is not
supported. Playback depends on the video's availability and host permissions.
Separate committee and board meetings may share one continuous recording.
When a meeting has a start time, **Starts at HH:MM:SS** shows the selected point.
The player and its watch link open there automatically; you can still rewind
or seek elsewhere in the recording.

Site [search](/search/) finds meetings by their recorded notes, transcripts,
motion subjects, motion details, and outcomes as well as their event details.
For example, search **FSS-2** to find the board meeting containing that motion.
Choose **Events** to limit results to calendar events and meetings. Open the
meeting result to read the notes and full voting record. Uploaded file contents
are searchable separately in the [public document archive](/documents/) when
those files have been published there.

Member names in meeting attendance, roll calls, notes, and vote details link to
their profiles. Each profile has a **Meeting votes** section with the member's choice,
motion, outcome, meeting date, and motion details. Select the meeting title to
open that exact vote and its full roll call. Use **Load more votes** for older
records. A profile with no recorded choices says **No meeting votes recorded here.**.
Public profiles list public meetings; unlisted meetings remain accessible from
their direct links. Admins can also see restricted meetings in voting records.

1. Open the [community calendar](/calendar/). Use the month arrows to find a date. Select a day to show its events, or switch to **Agenda** for a list of upcoming events.
2. Use **Organization**, **Calendar**, **Category**, or **Search events** to narrow the list. Meetings are labeled **Meeting**.
3. Select the event title to open its page. If records have been added, the **Meeting record** section shows notes or minutes, a transcript, attendance, votes, and downloadable files.

If you do not see meeting notes, they may not have been added yet. For an older meeting, go to its month and select its day; the default event list starts from today.

## Keep up with the calendar

Calendar times and election dates and deadlines are shown in Eastern Time (America/New_York), including daylight saving changes. Election-day hours show when local polls open and close. **Subscribe to calendar** adds an updating feed of public events to your calendar app. If that button does not open your app, expand **Use a subscription URL** and copy the address. **Download calendar** imports a one-time copy instead. Updates to a subscription depend on your calendar app's refresh schedule.

## Search and explore

- [Search Civic Pulse](/search/) by name or topic, or choose a **Content type** to browse without entering a search term. Results can include people, organizations, elections, articles, maps, and events.
- [Explore maps](/maps/) by choosing a map, then opening **Map settings** to turn layers on or off. Use **Search places, precincts or addresses** in **Places on your map**; select a place there or on the map to see its details. **Copy map link** shares the current layer selection.
- Browse [people](/people/) and [organizations](/orgs/) to find profiles and related information. Each directory has its own name search. In People, combine a name or keyword search with a **Type** filter (elected official, appointed official, candidate, or other). Sort by **First name** or **Last name**, A–Z or Z–A, then use **Previous** and **Next** to browse all matches in pages of 24. The result count and page number show your position. Changing search, type, or sort returns to page 1; **Clear** restores all types and first-name A–Z order. Last-name sorting uses the final word of the saved full name, ignoring common suffixes such as Jr., Sr., II, III, and IV; compound surnames follow that final word.
- Open [elections](/elections/) for available contests, candidates, and dates or deadlines. Select a date to open its calendar event.
  Election details default to **Bibb County races**, including statewide offices and districts covering part of the county. Choose **All recorded races** to see other races in the same election. Candidates outside Bibb County remain searchable; a search link to their race opens that race automatically. Your own ballot depends on your address.

If a search shows nothing, try a shorter term, another content type, or fewer calendar or map filters. Some records have not been added yet.

## Accounts and access

You do not need an account to browse public information. To create one, [create an account](/signup/) with your email and a password of at least eight characters. Complete the security check and confirm your email if prompted. After signing in, [Your account](/account/) shows your account details and lets you sign out. A regular account currently gives you the same content access as a public visitor.

**Unlisted** records can be opened with a direct link but do not appear in public listings or search. **Private** records require admin access. If a link says an admin account is required, signing in with a regular account will not open it.

Information may change. Confirm important meeting, voting, or service details with the organization that published them.

## Follow a records request

Open [Records requests](/requests/) to search Open Records Requests (ORR), federal
Freedom of Information Act (FOIA) requests, and other records requests. Filter by
stage or organization; organization profiles link to requests they receive or
originate. Open a request to read its timeline, fees, supporting files and news links.

The stage describes progress: draft, submitted, initial response, in progress,
appeal / mediation, or closed. Outcome is separate: not assessed, satisfactory,
partial, or unsatisfactory. A partial release can remain in progress. Follow-up
dates are manually recorded, not legal deadlines or automatic reminders.

Fees are recorded in USD as separate estimated, billed and paid entries. Totals
sum each category independently; estimates are not added to bills or payments.
No fees recorded does not establish that a request is free.

Public requests appear in the directory. Unlisted requests require their direct
link. Private requests are visible only to their assigned owner and admins.
Account ownership and transfer history are always private. Files can be private
even when a request is public; downloads use short-lived links.

Only admins create requests. If one is assigned to you, use **Account → Manage
your records requests**, then **Manage this request** to update it. You can add
updates, fees, links and files, change visibility, and transfer it to another
registered user using their Account-page user ID. A transfer gives the recipient
access to private files; the previous owner loses access unless they are an admin.
Saving records here does not submit a request or payment to an agency.

## Change history

Choose **View history** on an organization, person, election, map, event, office,
service record, candidacy, ballot question, or article to open its live history.
Open a revision to compare **Before** and **After**, with the edit time and the
editor’s display name. Relationship edits also appear in the owning record’s
history: for example, candidacy changes appear for the person and election.
Use **Load older changes** to continue through earlier revisions.

Anonymous visitors and regular signed-in users see the same history: revisions
must have been public when recorded, and the record and its referenced records
must still be public. Edits made while private or unlisted, including publication
transitions containing hidden earlier values, stay hidden. Public deletions can
remain visible; deleting a hidden record does not make its past history public.
Editor account IDs and email addresses are not shown in public history. Meeting
file storage paths are also hidden; use the meeting viewer to access files.

Tracking begins when this feature is enabled; edits before that point cannot be
reconstructed. Imports and maintenance without a signed-in editor are labeled
**System / import**. History updates immediately, even when the profile page still
shows the previous static build. History is for inspection; it does not restore
or roll back records.

## Civic Pulse short links

Shared links beginning with `https://www.civpulse.org/l/` redirect to a destination
chosen by a Civic Pulse admin. Scanning a Civic Pulse QR code opens the same short
URL. The destination may change while the shared URL and printed QR code stay the
same. No account or JavaScript is required to follow a short link.

A paused or unknown link displays a not-found message. A temporary service error
asks you to try again. Link owners can see aggregate redirect counts and the last
visit time, including repeated visits and bots; these statistics do not store
your IP address, browser identity, or cookies.

## Legislation

[Browse legislation](/legislation/) to search bills and resolutions by title or
identifier. Filter by **Jurisdiction** (state, federal, or not yet classified),
**State**, and **Legislative term**. State options reflect recorded coverage;
choosing federal clears and disables the state selector. Sort by introduction
date (newest or oldest) or title (A–Z or Z–A). Filters and sorting apply to the
entire result set, including **Load more**. Controls update results immediately;
use **Search** to submit keywords. Empty results do not mean a state has no bills:
only records added to Civic Pulse appear here.
Anonymous bill and session browsing uses the latest published snapshot; search,
filters, sorting and Load more work within that snapshot. New imports and edits
appear after publication. Signed-in browsing uses live access checks. Unlisted
records remain available only through their permitted direct links; private
records require administrator access.
[Sessions](/legislation/sessions/) groups
regular and special sessions, with links to their bills, actions and votes.

Public bill, vote and session details are published snapshots, including their
related records and vote counts. Use **Load more** for longer sections. Existing
public UUID links open the published page and retain section anchors. Signed-in
readers and **Open live record with access checks** use current permissions;
unlisted direct links and private administrator access retain their existing
rules. New imports and edits appear in public details after publication.

A bill record includes sponsorship history, documented positions, official text
versions, amendments, a timeline, votes and media coverage. Follow a vote to read
the exact question and each recorded member choice. A vote on an amendment or
procedure is different from a vote on final passage. Sponsorship and public support
are separate from voting choices.

People profiles show legislative voting history with legislative-term, session, chamber/committee, bill-title/identifier and
category filters, plus sponsorships. The office and district reflect recorded
service at the time. Votes are grouped by bill or resolution, showing only the
latest matching vote and the number of other matching votes by that person.
Counts and the latest vote follow the selected filters. Attendance roll calls are
excluded from profile history, entry totals, and per-bill vote counts. Their
underlying source records remain available through direct vote links.
Use **View bill details
and vote history** to see the bill's full recorded vote history, or follow the
latest vote question for its member choices. Bills with the same number in
different terms or numbering contexts stay separate. Votes without a visible
bill association are listed individually. Use **Previous votes** and **Next votes**
to move between groups.
Missing choices mean the record is unknown, not that the member
was absent. Voice votes may have an outcome without individual choices. Coverage
labels describe the imported record; official totals can differ from partial or
visibility-filtered member lists. Unlisted records require their direct links;
private records are available only to administrators.

Bill pages put the timeline and votes first, with section links to sponsorships,
text versions, media coverage and other records. Dates use readable month names;
exact vote times identify UTC. Member names link to profiles and chamber names
link to organizations. **View source** and **View history** remain alongside each
record.

On a vote page, **Official result** shows the reported yes/no/other totals.
**Partial member record** describes the imported choices available on Civic Pulse,
not the size of the official roll call. A person's **Voted Yea** label and the
vote's **Result: rejected** describe different things. Cloture is procedural and
must not be read as final passage. On profiles, expand **Filter legislative votes**
to narrow the record or use **Clear filters**. **Meeting votes** is a separate
section for votes recorded in local meeting minutes.

Long profile sections have independent page controls. **Legislative record**
shows 20 entries per page with **Previous votes** and **Next votes**; applying or
clearing filters returns to page 1. **News & articles** shows 10 articles per page
with its own previous/next buttons. Changing either section's page replaces that
section's cards and leaves the other sections in place. Meeting votes retain
**Load more votes** for earlier meeting records.

Profiles automatically retry brief connection failures. If votes or articles
still fail to load, select **Retry legislative history** or **Retry articles**.
A failed page request keeps the currently displayed votes until the retry succeeds.

Public people, organization and election directories use published snapshots.
Search and filters run in your browser; people still support type filters,
first/last name sorting and Previous/Next pages. Organization searches include
public members and roles; election searches include public candidates, contests
and ballot questions. Use quotes for phrases, `OR` for alternatives and `-word`
to exclude a word. Signed-in searches keep the existing permitted live view;
results and relevance order can differ from the published public snapshot.

## Read the published public record archive

Open [Public record archive](/records/) from the footer, or follow the published
archive link from Legislation, Legislative sessions, Community calendar or Records
requests. Choose a category and use **Next page** / **Previous page** to browse.
Individual bills, legislative votes, sessions, events/meetings and public requests
have stable links and readable content without JavaScript. A meeting page includes
available notes, transcripts, recorded attendance and choices; missing choices
are unknown, not absences. Coverage includes only records added to Civic Pulse.

Use **Open live record and available files** for the existing interactive viewer,
file downloads and authorized editing. Unlisted and private records do not appear
in the archive; existing shared viewer links keep their access rules. Published
pages change only after a rebuild, including removals and visibility changes.
Confirm current details with the original source.

Meeting attendance may say **Observed at a ballot only** when a named vote is the
available evidence; this does not establish attendance throughout the meeting.
**Attendance unknown** and **unknown** vote choices mean the source does not
support a stronger conclusion. “Vote Not Recorded” is never counted as No,
abstention, or absence. Recorded ballots can appear before an official outcome
is known. Anonymous public profile and event snapshots update after publication; signed-in
meeting viewers update live.

Public calendar browsing and event pages use published snapshots. Month navigation,
day selection, Today, Month/Agenda views, organization/calendar/category filters,
calendar checkboxes, colors and keyword filtering still work in your browser.
Public meeting notes, transcripts, attendance, votes and video settings publish
with the event. Attachment downloads still check access when clicked. Existing
shared event links continue to work; unlisted events stay accessible by their
shared links, and private events require an admin. Signed-in views retain live
access checks. Public calendar subscriptions update after publication and your
calendar app's next refresh.
Shared event cards link to the calendar copy carrying a meeting record when one
is available, so combining calendars does not hide the meeting details.

## Election results and turnout

Open [Election results and turnout](/elections/results/) from the Elections page,
choose an election and load its reports. **Show earlier reports** reveals how
reported totals changed. Each report identifies its area, counting group, ballot
style, time and unofficial/certified status. Reports can overlap: county summaries
already include precinct or machine ballots, so do not add them together.

Turnout uses reported voters, or ballots when voters are unavailable, divided by
the matching registered-voter count. The page labels which numerator it uses.
Contest participation can use a different denominator. Missing data says “Not
reported”; no reports does not mean zero votes. The November 3, 2026 election can
have turnout reports before election day, while May 19, 2026 is historical. These
are reported counts, not winner projections. Original photographs remain private
for admin review.

## Start from the homepage

Use **What would you like to find?** on the [homepage](/) to search by name,
organization, place, or topic. Select **Search** or press Enter to open the
search results, then narrow them by content type. An empty search opens the
search page so you can choose a type to browse.

**Elections at a glance** shows up to three upcoming published elections in date
order, including Election Day itself. Dates roll forward in Eastern Time; new
or edited election records appear after the next site publish. The homepage
also links to people, organizations, and public records, shows the public map
count, and lists upcoming community events.

The header's **Browse** menu groups all destinations under **Community**,
**Government**, and **Research & help**. Open it by click, tap, or keyboard;
press Escape to close it. **Calendar**, **Search**, and **Sign in** remain directly
available. Signed-in visitors see **Account**, and admins also see **Admin**.
Select the Civic Pulse logo to return home. The shorter footer contains
**About**, **Guides & help**, and **Search**; archive and directory links are
available in **Browse** on every page.

Election detail pages show **Voter turnout** charts when Georgia SOS structured
turnout is available. Choose a **County** (Bibb is selected when available) to
compare reported ballot styles and inspect absentee measures. Exact counts, source
as-of labels and the separate collection time accompany the charts. Each chart
has its own zero-based ballot scale. Missing counts say “Not reported”; overlapping
measures are not added, and no registered-voter denominator is inferred. Use
**All reports, source details & earlier snapshots** to inspect reporting history.
The charts load live and follow the election’s existing visibility rules.

The **Bibb County precinct turnout** map uses a fixed 0–100% scale: darker
precincts indicate higher participation. Gray means “Not reported,” while a
reported zero uses the lightest green. Select a precinct for counts and report
time/status, or expand **Precinct counts and percentages** for a keyboard-accessible
table. Choose a ballot style when separate reports are available; only all-method
precinct reports are used, never sums of overlapping reports or contest counts.
Rates need that precinct’s voters (or ballots) and registered-voter counts. The
November county aggregates and May precinct contest results do not currently
provide these denominators, so their precincts remain gray. County turnout is
never distributed across precincts. The map reuses the public Bibb boundary
snapshot; check boundary vintages before comparing historical elections.

Georgia SOS structured turnout also appears separately on the election results page.
Choose a turnout snapshot or load more county rows to inspect all available
measures and source locators. These are unofficial cumulative observations;
overlapping early, absentee and turnout tables must never be added together.
Display text and numeric values are preserved, unavailable values stay unavailable,
and an absent source time is labeled separately from collection time. Registration
and precinct denominators are not inferred. Reports update live after an import.

### Exploring election reports

On [Election results & turnout](/elections/results/), choose an election to start
with its county overview. Use **Reporting area** to select a precinct, then
**Voting method** and **Ballot style** to choose an available report. The page
shows one reporting scope at a time: county, precinct, and method reports overlap
and are never added together. Unavailable metrics say **Not reported**, not zero.

Search by contest or candidate name; expand a contest to see votes and its share
of the reported choice total. Source notes and detailed participation metrics are
under expandable reporting details. **Include earlier reports** shows past
snapshots for the selected scope separately. The URL retains your filters for
sharing. **Refresh reports** fetches the latest data without a site rebuild.
