# Homepage election callout

## Direction contract

THESIS: Help visitors find the ballot and key dates when voting approaches, with a timely callout above the homepage destinations.

OWN-WORLD: Preserve the existing cream, forest, sage and rust palette, Unna headings, DM Sans body, square composition and existing button language.

STORY: Visitors see the election name, Election Day and the first early-voting date, then open the election details.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A forest field between the introduction and maps/calendar, with election information left and a cream action right. Stack naturally on mobile. Multiple qualifying elections appear in date order.

FORM: A local extension of the existing homepage, without a visual-world change. No imagery or animation needed. Display begins seven calendar days before early voting and ends after Election Day in America/New_York. Only public elections and public key dates qualify; missing early-voting dates do not trigger a guessed window.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
